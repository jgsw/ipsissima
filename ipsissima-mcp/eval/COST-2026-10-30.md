# What a reconstruction costs, remeasured on 25 runs (30 October 2026)

*The August model (`COST-2026-08-27.md`) predates the mechanism layer (1 October) and the tables
(22 October), and the method has been reorganised since. This remeasures cost on the method as it
now stands, from the phase 3 trials of the extraction-materials review.*

## The runs

- **Which runs:** 25 reconstructions of five texts. Each is one fresh, top-level Claude Code
  session, with no subagents, metered from its own transcript.
- **The method:** each run used the method as of 9caf1db or later: NEW, FULL, S6, RA-NEW, 126 and
  126b in the trial logs.
- **Left out:**
  - the OLD arms, which used the method before the review;
  - the S7 arms, a reading order that was rejected;
  - one run resumed after a usage limit, whose re-cache inflates it.
- **Settings:** every arm used the same settings, inherited from the session that launched it.

**The measure** is what the run adds: new input plus output, less the context the session opened
with.
- **New input** is uncached input plus cache writes.
- **The opening context** is the client's own instructions and tool list, about 75,000 tokens in
  these sessions. It is left out because it belongs to the client, not to the method. Another
  client, or the same one with fewer connectors, opens smaller or larger.
- **Cache reads** carry the conversation from one call to the next. They are reported separately
  below, because they depend on how many round trips a run makes more than on anything in the
  method.

| text | source | runs | map | added by the run |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gettier 1963 | 1,519 words | 1 | 14 KB | 94k |
| Shepherd 2025 | 3,760 words | 1 | 32 KB | 193k |
| Bates et al. 2011 | 6,731 words | 11 | 38–53 KB | 178–243k (mean 213k) |
| Stone 1989 | 10,176 words | 1 | 65 KB | 305k |
| Valentino et al. 2018 | 10,857 words | 11 | 33–43 KB | 191–313k (mean 246k) |

**Least squares over the 25:**

```
tokens added  ≈  48,000  +  2.2 × bytes of map  +  10.7 × words of source
```

The fit:
- R² is 0.73;
- the residual standard deviation is 24,000;
- the largest residual is 55,000 (Valentino RA-NEW-2).

**Output alone**, the thinking and the writing, is `21,000 + 0.98 × map bytes + 3.5 × source
words` (R² 0.74). On the twelve runs of the method as it now ships, it averages 90,000.

## What changed since August, and what cannot be compared

**The method is larger.**
- It now reads the mechanism and tables documents with every argument: about 34,000 words always
  read, against the August model's 68,640 bytes, which is about 17,200 tokens.
- Every map carries frames and, usually, a mechanism.
- The check reply was cut, from about 14.5k tokens a round to 1.2–1.7k while a fault remains. The
  census is now sent once, in prose.

**The measures differ.**
- August took its totals from subagents' own summaries.
- These are top-level sessions metered turn by turn, with the opening context subtracted.
- So the old and new constants are not the same quantity, and their difference is not a trend.

What can be said:
- **Articles:** the August model's predictions for these articles fall within about a quarter of
  what these runs add (Bates 203k against 213k; Valentino 205k against 246k; Stone 227k against 305k).
- **Short texts:** it overstates them badly. It predicts 163k for Gettier, against 94k measured.

**The source term is larger and less certain.** August's 2.3 tokens a word rested on two runs of
one book. This one's 10.7 rests on five texts, and two of them carry 22 of the 25 runs. Source
length and the density of a text's argument move together here, so read the term as "longer
articles cost more", not as a price per word. **Do not extrapolate it past about 11,000 words**:
no text here is longer.

## What a run's cost is made of

Bates and Valentino, the method as it now ships (12 runs):

| | per run |
|---|---|
| the session's opening context (client, not method) | about 78k |
| added by the run (new input + output) | 179k–262k |
| of which output (thinking and writing) | 73k–109k |
| cache reads (carrying the conversation) | 1.4M–2.4M at a tenth of the rate |
| rate-weighted total, on the published multipliers (1-hour cache writes 2×, reads 0.1×) | 636k–808k (mean 706k) |

**The rate-weighted total is mostly cache reads, and they follow turns, not text.** Across the
25 runs the rate-weighted total fits the map and the source poorly (R² 0.34). The two dearest runs
are Valentino's with 19 and 24 turns, at 1.05M and 1.12M, against 0.66–0.89M for its other runs.

**August's three levers, now:**
- **Effort:** not remeasured.
- **Round trips:** still the bulk of the rate-weighted total, through cache reads.
- **The cache's lifetime:** every session here wrote one-hour cache entries. None expired, so no
  turn re-cached the context.

## Falsifiers

- A text past 11,000 words that costs less than the source term predicts. The term is the least
  certain part of the model.
- A run of the current method on a short text (under 2,000 words) far from 95k. Gettier is one run.
- A run whose output differs from `21k + 0.98 × bytes + 3.5 × words` by more than about 25k with
  the same number of check rounds.

**The data:**
- The runs, sessions and logs are in the trial folder (not in this repository). It is listed in
  TRIAL-LOG.md there, with the protocols PROTOCOL-S6, PROTOCOL-S7 and PROTOCOL-RA.
- The meter is `run_cost.py` here. The fit is a least-squares fit over the per-run totals, by the
  same arithmetic.
